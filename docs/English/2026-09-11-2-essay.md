# Our essay

In recent times there is a lot of talk about screen time of teenagers and kids. Some think that they spend inappropriately much time on their devices. Those people reckon that parents should be shortening that time for their children. Others view reducing electronics usage as limiting freedom of the youth.

The side opposing free use of devices argue that teens wouldn't know when it is enough and use their electronics all day. They also claim that the youth would devalue real world and relations in it. Therfore, as they think, teens and younger kids need to be told when to stop and have limits set on their usage.

Other people believe that children when provided with unlimited access will be able to control themselves, and by giving them no limits, they might use their devices less than with restrictions. That is because they won't feel like they need to get the most out of their usage. Those people also claim that young people would use their devices for educational purposes, but when limited they will not "waste" their limited time on such things.

In conclusion, it can be seen that both sides have their reasons. On the one hand, some teens might abuse unlimited usage given them in good will. On the other hand, limiting their time can lead to increasing amount of it they spend daily and decreasing the possiblity they will do something productive.
